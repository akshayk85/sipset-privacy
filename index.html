Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

The short version

Everything you put into Sipset stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no servers of ours and no ads. We never see your data.

What Sipset stores, on your phone only

Your profile (age range, optional factors such as smoking or pregnancy, bedtime and wake time), the drinks you log, custom sizes, sleep times and how you rated them, plans, and your settings. It lives in a database inside the app and is deleted when you uninstall Sipset.

Microphone and voice

If you use voice logging, Sipset uses your phone's on-device speech recognition. Audio is turned into text on the phone, is not recorded or saved, and is never sent anywhere by Sipset.

Health Connect

If you connect Health Connect, Sipset reads your sleep sessions only, to show sleep in Insights and to tune your caffeine estimates. It never writes to Health Connect, never reads other health data, and keeps what it reads on your phone. You can remove access at any time in Health Connect.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent through a server.

Sharing

When you share a card, Sipset creates the image on your phone and hands it to the app you choose. What happens next is up to that app.

Purchases

If you buy Sipset Pro, the payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store. Sipset only learns that you have Pro.

Children

Sipset is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Not medical advice

Caffeine numbers in Sipset are estimates for general wellbeing, not medical advice or diagnosis.
